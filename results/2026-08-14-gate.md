## Protocol traps hit (both cost a silent wrong number)

1. **lm-eval silently ignores `num_fewshot` for wmdp_bio.** `evaluator.py:327`: the task yaml
   pins `num_fewshot: 0`, so `simple_evaluate(num_fewshot=5)` is discarded with only a
   logger.info. Our first k=5 cells were 0-shot in disguise — bit-identical to 14 dp across k.
   Fix: `task.set_config(key="num_fewshot", value=k)` on the task object + assert it stuck +
   pass the task dict through. Verified: context 365 → 2280 chars at k=5.
2. **Shots come from the test split** — wmdp ships no train/validation split, so lm-eval
   falls back to scoring a pool the shots were drawn from. lm-eval's fallback, not a paper
   protocol. Carry as a caveat on the k=5 number.
